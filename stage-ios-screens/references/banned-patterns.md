# Banned patterns

If any of these appear, remove them before delivering. They are the usual tells of an AI-generated app UI.

## Chrome and layout

- Gradient-blob, mesh, or aurora backgrounds behind the app or the presentation canvas
- Generic purple, indigo, or violet as the accent (including “intelligent AI” lilac)
- Glassmorphism on every card, or frosted panels stacked on a blurred photo
- Card-in-card: a rounded rectangle nested in another rounded rectangle with two shadows
- Four identical stat cards in a 2×2 grid, especially with a circular progress ring in each
- Everything centered: centered body copy, centered lists, centered transaction rows
- Floating capsule tab bars with drop shadows plus a second bottom bar
- Hamburger menus on iPhone, or a web navbar inside the device
- Tiny gray text that drops below Caption 1, or light-gray-on-white body copy that fails contrast
- Huge rounded search bars as the only header, with no navigation bar
- Onboarding carousels, progress dots, or “Get started” as one of the main product shots
- A marketing landing page, a laptop browser, or a campaign-style scroll story presented as an app screen

## Content tells

- “Welcome back, User!”, “Welcome back, John!”, or a waving-hand greeting as the home header
- Lorem ipsum, “John Doe”, “Jane Smith”, “User”, “ethan.b@example.com”, “Company Inc.”
- Perfectly round numbers: `$99`, `$100`, `$1,000`, `10,000 steps`, `50%`, `12:00`, `5.00 km`
- Fake charts with no axes, no units, and no dates; rainbow doughnuts; 3D bars
- Emoji used as interface icons, category marks, or tab symbols
- Stock avatars, invented Unsplash or Picsum URLs, or identical faces repeated
- Badge piles: `NEW`, `HOT`, `LIVE`, `AI`, and numeric red badges on every tab
- Empty states with a crying illustration and “Oopsie”; error states that say “Something went wrong” and nothing else
- Settings screens full of lorem toggles as a hero shot

## Type and data

- Inter (or any web font) as the *primary* face when the system stack would work; display serifs or three font families in the UI
- Random font sizes that are not in the Dynamic Type table
- Fake SF Symbols as icon fonts that don’t render, squares, or tofu
- Mixed stroke weights in the SVG icon set, or filled and outline icons used inconsistently in the same tab bar
- Copy that sounds like a pitch deck: unlock, seamless, elevate, next-gen, your personal AI companion

## Device and canvas

- Android system bars, or an iPhone with no status bar, no island or notch, and no home indicator
- A notch on a Dynamic Island frame, or a punch-hole camera
- Devices at inconsistent scales, rotated for decoration, overlapping with a messy stack, or wrapped onto two rows
- Neon glow around the bezel, a fancy wallpaper behind the row, or a Dribbble wordmark cooked into the canvas
- Status bar time other than 9:41, or a battery icon drawn as the letters “100”

If the brief genuinely needs something close to a banned item (a real live badge, a real 2×2 of different metrics), keep it specific, uneven, and in system chrome, and never use it as decoration.
